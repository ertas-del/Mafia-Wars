# PATCH-001: Also rename Santoro and Russo

**Reason.** At spec approval the user also asked to swap the two surnames flagged under Risks. Santoro and Russo were both names of real Commission-era mob figures. This patch rides with plan approval. It changes only the specification's text and adds no new component.

**Affected task:** T-001 only (the cast data and its denylist test). No task has started yet.

## Specification

### D3
- **Before:** In this game's copy, the surnames Vitale, Amato and Carbone are replaced with invented ones. Nicknames and roles stay.
- **After:** In this game's copy, the surnames Vitale, Amato, Carbone, Santoro and Russo are replaced with invented ones. Nicknames and roles stay.

### Depth and Defaults, "Replacement surnames" row
- **Before:** Lorenzo "The Ledger" Ravasi, Frankie "Photo Finish" Cardella and Johnny "Runway" Lomarco.
- **After:** Lorenzo "The Ledger" Ravasi, Frankie "Photo Finish" Cardella, Johnny "Runway" Lomarco, Gino "Velvet" Fiorella and Mikey "Nine Lives" Castagna.

### AC-001 denylist
- **Before:** The denylist includes Vitale, Amato, Carbone, Gigante, Gotti, Castellano, Persico, Indelicato, Ruggiero, Casso, Gambino, Genovese, Lucchese, Bonanno and Colombo.
- **After:** The denylist includes Vitale, Amato, Carbone, Santoro, Russo, Gigante, Gotti, Castellano, Persico, Indelicato, Ruggiero, Casso, Gambino, Genovese, Lucchese, Bonanno and Colombo.

### Risks and Alternatives, "Other surnames"
- **Before:** some common surnames in the 50 also belong to real people of the era, for example Santoro and Russo. Only the three in D3 are renamed. The rest are flagged at approval.
- **After:** some common surnames in the 50 also belong to real people of the era. The five in D3 are renamed. Any others are common names and stay.

## Applied note
